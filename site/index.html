How it works

Each evening the strategy decides what to hold; trades fill at the next morning's open with modelled fees and slippage. The active sleeve's settings are chosen each year using only earlier data. A model is considered for paper trading only if it passes the promotion gate. Code, tests and the decision log are in the repository.
